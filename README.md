**Q: My device says "Blocked by Play Protect"**

**Q: The app isn't appearing on my home screen**
A: After installation, access Focus from your app drawer. You can then set it as your default launcher through your device settings.

## 🎯 How to Set Focus as Your Default Launcher

1. Open your device's **Settings**
2. Navigate to **Apps** or **Applications**
3. Find **Default apps** or **Home app**
4. Select **Focus** from the list
5. You'll now see Focus every time you press your home button

## 📱 Making the Most of Focus

### Setting Up App Timers

- Open Focus and tap on "App Timers"
- Select which apps to limit
- Choose your daily time limit for each app
- Focus will automatically lock the app when the time expires

### Viewing Your Weekly Report

- After seven days of use, open Focus
- Tap "Weekly Review" to see your usage summary
- Use this data to identify your digital habits
- Adjust your app timers accordingly
